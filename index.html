Top Story

Russian Drone Strikes Train Near Ukraine-Poland Border, Narrowly Missing Diplomatic Convoy

A Russian drone struck a train at the Yahodyn border station on Sunday — just minutes after a diplomatic train carrying former British PM Boris Johnson and former Swedish PM Carl Bildt had departed ahead of schedule. A second train carrying former CIA Director David Petraeus was still at the station; it was evacuated as the drone approached. No casualties reported.

"It is highly probable that the Russian drone's intended target was in fact the diplomatic train, which had departed the station earlier than scheduled." — Ukrzaliznytsia (Ukraine state rail)

The near-miss has amplified calls across NATO capitals for stronger air-defense corridors along Ukraine's western rail links. Poland scrambled interceptor jets in response.

Source: Washington Post, NPR, NBC News
